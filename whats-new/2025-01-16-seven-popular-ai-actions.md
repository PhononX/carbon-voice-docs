# Seven popular AI Actions

People are getting a lot out of AI Actions, so here are the seven most popular ones based
on real usage data — what they're good for, and what each actually does.

<!-- truncate -->

## 1. Bulleted Summary

Most popular by a distance.

**Use it for** turning a long message that arrived while you were in a meeting into a
clear bulleted list, so you can focus on the key ideas without losing the whole thought.
Or for turning a brainstorm memo into crisp points to follow up on.

**How it works:** extracts the details and points while dropping filler words, leaving
something scannable.

## 2. Catch-up, Improve Structure, and Clean-up Transcript

A group of summary actions that all give you a faster way to consume messages.

**Use them for** seeing what you missed in a long, active conversation (**Catch-up**),
polishing the wording (**Clean-up Transcript**), or reorganizing the ideas (**Improve
Structure**).

**How they work:** Catch-up summarizes across multiple messages with takeaways and action
items. Improve Structure groups related concepts for better flow. Clean-up corrects
grammar and removes filler while keeping the message's flow.

## 3. To-do List

**Use it for** capturing follow-ups on the walk between meetings.

**How it works:** scans for actionable items and turns them into an organized, prioritized
task list.

Use the [Zapier integration](/integrations/zapier) to feed the list into your task
manager automatically.

## 4. Presentation Outline

**Use it for** talking through a slide overview and getting a slide-ready outline back in
minutes, suggested visuals included.

**How it works:** builds a structured outline with titles, key points, and visuals, slide
by slide.

The [Google Slides add-on](https://www.getcarbon.app/google-add-on/slides) turns that
outline into actual slides.

## 5. Professional Email

**Use it for** the polished email you need to send when all you have is a rough sense of
what to say.

**How it works:** takes your voice notes and turns them into a clear, professional email.

## 6. LinkedIn Post

**Use it for** the insight that arrives while you're out — capture it, then refine it into
something with clarity and impact.

**How it works:** formats your ideas into a structured post, with hashtag suggestions and
an engaging tone.

## 7. Brainstorm More Ideas

Proof that AI can do more than reformat your ideas — it can help make them better.

**Use it for** when you're stuck on a project and need fresh directions.

**How it works:** analyzes the context of your discussion and generates relevant ideas to
build on.
