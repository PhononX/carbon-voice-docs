# Introducing listen-only conversations

**Listen-only conversations** let people listen in without the ability to post or reply.

<!-- truncate -->

## What it's for

**📣 Team broadcasts.** Leadership can create a listen-only conversation for company-wide
announcements without accidental replies cluttering an important update — especially useful
on larger teams.

**🔒 Private communities.** Good for customer communities, coaches, or external advocates
who want to share quick updates like a private podcast, without clients posting back or
seeing each other.

## New options in conversation settings

- **Set as listen-only.** Mark any conversation as listen-only and new members join as
  listeners by default. Listen-only members can't see other listen-only members.
- **Control who can post.** Promote an individual from listener to speaker to manage who
  contributes while everyone else listens.
- **Flexible sharing.** This works with every way of joining a conversation — add people
  manually, let anyone in your workspace find and join, or share a public link.
