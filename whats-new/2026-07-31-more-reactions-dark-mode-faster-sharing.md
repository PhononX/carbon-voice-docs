# More reactions, dark mode, faster sharing

We've been shipping a steady stream of improvements based on your feedback. Here are a
few you may have missed.

<!-- truncate -->

## More reactions

Message reactions are no longer a fixed set — you can react using more than 1,400
emoji.

![The emoji reaction picker with a full emoji set](/img/whats-new/more-reactions.webp)

## See who reacted

Press and hold on a reaction, or right-click on desktop, to see everyone who reacted.

![The list of people who reacted to a message](/img/whats-new/who-reacted.webp)

## Dark mode

Dark mode follows your phone settings, and you can make your own choice in settings.

![Carbon Voice in dark mode](/img/whats-new/dark-mode.webp)

## Select part of a transcript on desktop

Highlight and copy just the text you need from a transcript — useful for grabbing
quotes, notes, or action items.

![Selecting part of a transcript on desktop](/img/whats-new/select-transcript-desktop.webp)

## Faster voice memo sharing

When you create a voice memo, a shareable link is copied to your clipboard
automatically, along with customizable text.

![A shareable link copied automatically after recording a voice memo](/img/whats-new/faster-voice-memo-sharing.webp)

## Embed voice messages on a website

Embed a voice memo and its transcript on any website, blog, or Notion page. Create a
shareable link and paste it into Notion as an embed, or take the embed code from
message details.

![An embedded voice message with its transcript on a web page](/img/whats-new/embed-voice-message.webp)

## Sync with Obsidian

Sync your voice memos and conversations into Obsidian using the
[Carbon Voice Sync plugin](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/carbon-voice-sync).

![Voice memos synced into an Obsidian vault](/img/whats-new/obsidian-sync.webp)

## Move conversations

Created a conversation before you had a workspace? You can now move it into one — open
the conversation menu (**⋯**) and select **Move Conversation**.

![The Move Conversation option in the conversation menu](/img/whats-new/move-conversation.webp)

## Mark conversations as read from one page

Your unread list is easier to manage: mark conversations as read without opening each
one.

## Faster across the board

Performance improvements throughout the app, with the web experience noticeably
quicker to load.
