# Create your own AI prompts

You can now write your own AI prompts to turn conversations and voice memos into whatever
structured output you need.

<!-- truncate -->

To create and save one:

1. Go to any conversation.
2. Tap the AI Action icon in the lower right.
3. On the bottom row, tap **+** on the left.
4. Enter a title and your prompt, then save.

Your custom prompt then works on any message or set of messages, right alongside the 40+
built-in prompts.

You can also use a custom prompt as a [Zapier](/integrations/zapier) trigger — select
**User** as the type and your prompt appears in the list.
