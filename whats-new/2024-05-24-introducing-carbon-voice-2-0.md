# Introducing Carbon Voice 2.0

Carbon Voice 2.0 is here, redesigned around your feedback. Three things headline it:
**voice memos**, a **streamlined interface**, and **faster playback**.

<!-- truncate -->

📺 [Watch: a walk-through of what's new](https://www.loom.com/share/500ef6dfb54147eeaa432ab6b7e46107)

## Voice memos: a faster way to capture and share audio

A big record button now sits front and center, so you can capture an idea with a single
tap. When you're done, you can:

- **Get a shareable link** to save in a doc or send by email, text, or Slack. Recipients
  can listen inline in their messaging app or in a browser, and reply.
- **Share it to a conversation** as a new message once you've perfected your thoughts.
- **Listen later or get a transcript** to paste into AI tools or documents.

Some of the ways early testers are already using them:

- An intro message shared as a link in **sales outreach**, so prospects can listen and
  reply to start a conversation.
- **Quick thoughts to the team** dropped as a link into Slack and other chats.
- **Brainstorms** captured and sent to ChatGPT for structuring before sharing.
- **Bug reports** captured on the go, then turned into a well-structured ticket.
- **Dictated emails** transcribed, cleaned up, and sent.

## A streamlined messaging experience

The interface is more familiar and more intuitive, without giving up what makes audio
powerful. A few pointers on where things are:

- Start a **voice memo** with the big mic button in the bottom center.
- **Pull down** to search.
- **Conversations** are on the bottom left, with your **voice memos** just to the right.
- **Settings** are on the far right, next to the notification inbox.
- **Start a message to a conversation** by swiping left to right on it.
- **Tap the new message badge** to play all new messages, or those in one conversation.
- **Start a new conversation** with the floating **+**.

Inside a conversation:

- **Swipe right to left** on a message for the full transcript, labels, and more.
- **Swipe left to right** to reply as a thread.

## Faster playback on low-network connections

Cached audio means you can breeze through messages and stay understandable even when
your connection isn't good.

All of this sits on top of recent additions you may have missed:
