# New ways to manage how messages are displayed

Want your AI summaries alongside the original message? Prefer to hide audio transcripts
so more fits on screen? New preferences let you see a conversation the way that works
best for you.

<!-- truncate -->

> Release version 1.2.16484.

📺 [Watch: managing how your messages are displayed](https://www.loom.com/share/30ba44e62acb4f3faa1c73d1a24a1f42)

Also in this release, based on your feedback:

- More control over how messages are displayed, with options for AI summaries.
- The player no longer covers the text reply field.
- A cleaner conversation feed:
  - Dates sit on messages instead of on separators throughout the feed.
  - Message type is identifiable by the presence of the waveform.
  - Duration moved next to the waveform.
  - A **⋯** menu gets you to message details and the transcript for audio messages.
- Fixed an issue where the app didn't always notice it had been logged out.
