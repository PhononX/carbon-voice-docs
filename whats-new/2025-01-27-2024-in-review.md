# 2024 in review

Your feedback drove some big changes in 2024. Here's the short version of what happened.

<!-- truncate -->

**Carbon Voice 2.0.** We [refreshed the app](./2024-05-24-introducing-carbon-voice-2-0.md)
around voice memos for capturing ideas for yourself, and streamlined the whole
conversation experience.

**AI Actions.** [AI Actions](/ai/transform-with-ai-actions) arrived, turning any voice memo
or message into something you can act on.

**Catch-up.** [Catch-up](./2024-11-08-catch-up-ai-actions-on-multiple-messages-and-more.md)
added a quick way to summarize the messages you missed.

**New ways to capture and reach your messages:**

- The [Apple Watch app](./2024-11-26-carbon-voice-on-apple-watch.md)
- [Google Apps integrations](https://www.getcarbon.app/google-add-on) for Gmail, Docs,
  Sheets, and Slides
- The [Slack app](./2024-11-07-slack-and-google-sheets-integrations.md)
- [Zapier](/integrations/zapier)
