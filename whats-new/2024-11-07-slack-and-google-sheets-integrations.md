# Slack and Google Sheets integrations

Two new ways to get at your voice memos and conversations from the tools you're already
in: **Slack** and **Google Sheets**.

<!-- truncate -->

## Slack integration

[Try the Slack app](https://www.getcarbon.app/slackapp). It makes moving back and forth
between Slack and Carbon Voice quick:

- Notifications for new messages.
- Preview shared Carbon Voice messages right in Slack, where you can read, reply, or
  adjust permissions.
- Start discussions and async meetings in Carbon Voice from Slack with `/cv`.
- Auto-post messages and AI summaries from Carbon Voice conversations into Slack channels.

## Google Sheets integration

[Try the Google Sheets add-on](https://www.getcarbon.app/google-add-on/sheets) — the first
of several planned Google Apps features.

People often want to pull parts of a conversation out for notes or to build something
else, which until now meant selecting messages, copying the transcript, and pasting it
elsewhere. The add-on imports memos and messages from inside Google Sheets instead.

1. [Install the extension](https://www.getcarbon.app/google-add-on/sheets), or in Google
   Sheets go to **Extensions → Add-ons → Get Add-ons** and search for "Carbon Voice".
2. Select a conversation, or your voice memos.
3. Specify a date range, up to 30 days.
4. Import into the sheet.
