# Press and hold to record, AI Bullets, and an iMessage app

Another round of features for capturing, organizing, and sharing your voice messages.

<!-- truncate -->

📺 [Watch: what's new](https://youtu.be/s2JVRQu4PM8)

## Press and hold to record

Press and hold the mic button to start recording, and release to send instantly. If
single-tap recording has been creating accidental recordings for you, there's now a
preference to turn it off: **Profile Menu → Settings → Recording**.

## AI Bullets

Turn a longer voice message into clear, concise bullet points — useful for summarizing
information or organizing spoken content into something easier to scan.

![A voice message converted into bullet points](/img/whats-new/ai-bullets.webp)

1. **Swipe right to left** on any message card to open message info.
2. Toggle **Bullet Off** to convert the message into bullet points.

## iMessage app extension

Record and send Carbon Voice messages from inside iMessage, so all your audio stays in
one place:

1. In iMessage, tap the **+** icon.
2. Swipe up to find the Carbon Voice extension.
3. Record your message.
4. Send it as a link, or straight into an existing Carbon Voice conversation.

Everything you record there syncs to your Carbon Voice app automatically.
