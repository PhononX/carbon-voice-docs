# Carbon Voice for Mac is here

**One keystroke to skip the meeting.**

You already know async voice works. Today we made it faster. Carbon Voice for Mac is
live, and the desktop experience is a fundamentally new level of speed to talk.

<!-- truncate -->

📺 [Watch: capture and share ideas from anywhere](https://youtu.be/cQF5CwY7k8Q)

## Global keyboard shortcut: Option + V

Press **Option + V** from anywhere on your Mac. Talk. Get a shareable link instantly,
and send it anywhere you'd send a meeting invite.

That's it — faster than opening a calendar, faster than typing it out.

## Talk to your team quickly

![Carbon Voice running on macOS](/img/whats-new/mac-desktop.webp)

Press **Option + C** from anywhere on your Mac to message or talk to any existing
conversation, and use the cutter to quickly attach screenshots.

## What's new on desktop

- **Option + V** anywhere on your Mac to record and share instantly
- Press and hold **Space** to record inside a conversation
- **Cmd + K** to search across everything
- **Cmd + N** to start a new conversation
- Screenshot and attachment support

Everything syncs across mobile, web, and desktop, so your conversations and voice memos
go wherever you go.
