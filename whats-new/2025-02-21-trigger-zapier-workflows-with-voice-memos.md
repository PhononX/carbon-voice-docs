# Trigger Zapier workflows with voice memos

Recording a voice memo can now kick off a [Zapier workflow](/integrations/zapier) — a new
trigger alongside the existing ones for new messages in conversations, new AI results, and
new labels on messages.

<!-- truncate -->

It's a good fit for pushing the transcript of every voice memo into a Notion doc,
Evernote, Quip, Google Docs, Obsidian, or anywhere else you keep things.

## Filter by voice memo folder

You can optionally filter by voice memo folder, so a different workflow runs depending on
where you recorded:

- **To-do items** → Todoist, Things, Asana
- **Email drafts** → Gmail, Outlook, Google Docs
- **Blog post ideas** → Google Docs, Notion
- **Sales meeting updates** → Salesforce, Pipedrive, Notion, Google Sheets

See what's possible at [getcarbon.app/zapier](https://getcarbon.app/zapier), or build your
own Zap.
