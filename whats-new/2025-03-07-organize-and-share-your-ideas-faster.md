# Organize and share your ideas faster

Voice memos got two things in the latest app store release: faster sharing, and real
organization with labels and folders.

<!-- truncate -->

## Faster to share your ideas

The voice memo **ready** screen now gives you one-tap access to:

- 💬 Send the memo to a colleague in a conversation, or as a link.
- ✂️ Copy the transcript to your clipboard.
- ✨ Use AI to transform the transcript into bullets, an email, a presentation outline, or
  more.

![The voice memo ready screen with one-tap sharing options](/img/whats-new/voice-memo-ready-screen.webp)

## Labels

Ever scroll a long list of memos looking for one? Labels help you keep track of the
important ones, and labeled memos show up in your feed for quick access.

1. Tap a voice memo to open more options.
2. Select a default label, or create your own by tapping **+**.
3. Find all labeled memos in your list.

AI Actions appear in your list too, so you can see at a glance which AI tools you've used
across your memos.

## Folders

On a **paid plan**, you can create folders and keep everything sorted by workspace:

1. Go to your list of voice memos and tap the **folder icon** in the bottom right.
2. Choose which workspace to create the folder in.
3. Name it and start organizing.

To move a memo, press and hold it, then select **More → Move** and choose a folder. Or
swipe left to right on a folder to record straight into it.

See [Organizing voice memos](/voice-memos/organizing-voice-memos) for the full details.
