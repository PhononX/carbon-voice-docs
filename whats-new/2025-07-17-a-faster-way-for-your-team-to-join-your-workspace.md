# A faster way for your team to join your workspace

You can now connect your company domain to a workspace, so anyone who logs in with a
matching email address discovers it — no links to hunt for and no invites to track down.

<!-- truncate -->

## How it works

Once you've connected your company domains to your workspace, anyone logging in with a
matching email domain is told the workspace exists. You choose what happens next:

- They **join automatically**.
- They're **prompted to request to join**, and a workspace admin approves it.
- They see **custom instructions** you've written — internal process notes, or links for
  joining.

## Setting it up for a new workspace

[Create a workspace](/workspaces/create-a-workspace). Step 2 of creation walks you through
associating your company domains and choosing the setting.

## Setting it up for an existing workspace

1. Open your **Profile Menu** in the bottom-right corner.
2. Tap **Spaces**, then tap the **gear icon** to the right of your workspace.
3. Select **Admin Controls → Workspace Discovery**.
4. Tap **+** to add a domain.
5. Choose how people with that domain can join:
   - **Do not inform** — manual adds only
   - **Auto-join**
   - **Request to join**
   - **Show custom instructions**

See [Connect a domain](/workspaces/connect-a-domain) for the full documentation.
