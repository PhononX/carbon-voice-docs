# Connect ChatGPT to Carbon Voice

You can now connect ChatGPT to Carbon Voice. Capture a voice memo on the go, then open
ChatGPT and ask it to "turn my last voice memo into a blog post." Or turn a whole
conversation into structured documentation: "Turn our conversation 'Acme Investment' into an
investment memo."

## Setting up the connection

1. Go to **Apps and Connections** in ChatGPT settings.
2. Hit **Create** in the top right. You may need to turn on **Advanced Settings → Developer
   Mode** from the bottom of the page first.
3. Add **Carbon Voice** with the URL `https://mcp.carbonvoice.app`.
4. Accept the disclaimers and you're ready.

Two videos that help: [accessing Carbon Voice in
ChatGPT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OR728zFpP6Q) and the [ChatGPT admin guide for
turning on access](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kxpbtK68RwM).

## Using it

Type `@carbon` in the input field to ask about information from Carbon Voice. A few ideas:

- *"Turn my last voice memo into a blog post."*
- *"Turn the discussion in the 'Marketing Plan' conversation into a draft plan."*
- *"Based on the discussion in 'Daily Standup', what is everyone working on?"*
- *"Which conversations is Fred talking about MCP servers in?"*

## Works with other AI agents

Using Claude, Cursor, or something else? See the [MCP documentation](/integrations/mcp) for

with AI about a conversation without leaving Carbon Voice at all.

See [ChatGPT](/integrations/mcp/chatgpt) for the full documentation.
