# The first async podcast

We love hearing how Carbon Voice lets conversations take a new shape once scheduling and
coordination stop being constraints. Usually that's about eliminating calls and meetings.
Sometimes it's a kind of conversation that wasn't possible before — which is what this is.

<!-- truncate -->

## The idea

Ari Meisel had an observation: podcasts are often performative, because the time constraints
of the format push interviewees toward rehearsed answers instead of giving them room to think
and explore an idea properly. He
[wrote about the async podcast](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/asynchronous-podcast-why-future-deep-conversation-might-ari-meisel-ezyic/)
— a conversation captured over time in Carbon Voice.

His first guest was Rick Smith, CEO of Axon. They covered the story of Axon, the technology,
the approach to AI, and a lot of lessons about leadership and scale — nearly three hours of
content over 30 days.

## Listen

## Other ways to use this structure

People have already asked about adapting it:

- **Internal sharing** — two team members discussing a new feature or launch, shared with
  everyone else, like an internal podcast.
- **Closed external sharing** — like a public podcast, but only for customers you add.

More are in the works.
