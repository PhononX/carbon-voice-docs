# Stop scheduling, start reminding

**Reminders** are a new way to keep your team in sync, async.

The fastest way to cut meeting time isn't canceling meetings — it's replacing them with
something that works better, and for most teams that starts with the daily stand-up. Teams
using Carbon Voice for daily updates are already cutting meeting time by up to 60%.

<!-- truncate -->

The hard part was never the tool; it was the habit. Meetings are a forcing function, and when
the calendar invite disappears, so does the nudge to actually share an update. That's what
Reminders fix. Pair them with engagement stats and you have the nudge and the accountability,
without the meeting.

## How it works

A reminder posts a scheduled message into any conversation automatically. Your team gets
notified, everyone responds when it suits them, and the update happens without anyone having
to remember to start it.

![A reminder posting a scheduled prompt into a conversation](/img/whats-new/reminders-how-it-works.webp)

## How to set one up

It takes a few taps.

![Creating a reminder from conversation settings](/img/whats-new/reminders-setup.webp)

- **New conversations** — tap **Reminders** at the top of the conversation information block.
- **Existing conversations** — tap the conversation name to open Settings, scroll to
  **Reminders**, and create one there.

## What teams are replacing

**Daily stand-up.** *"What are you working on today?"* — posts every morning at 9am.

**Weekly 1:1.** *"How are you? Wins, blockers, and what do you need from me?"* — every Friday
afternoon.

**Weekly kickoff.** *"What do you want to accomplish this week? Do you need anything from
anyone?"* — every Monday at 8am.

**Week review.** *"What got done this week, and what's carrying over?"* — every Friday at 4pm.

**Client check-in.** *"Project status update."* — the 15th of every month keeps stakeholders on
a cadence.

**Team rituals.** Monday motivation, Friday wins, end-of-month reflections, appreciation
callouts.
