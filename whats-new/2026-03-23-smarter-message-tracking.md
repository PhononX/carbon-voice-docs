# Smarter message tracking

This update reworks how you find new messages and mark them read, upgrades replying with a
saved recording, and brings a batch of performance work.

<!-- truncate -->

📺 [Watch: finding new messages and Listen Later](https://youtube.com/shorts/KrBqYrqSVmo)

## The What's New line

A recurring piece of feedback was that messages didn't seem to clear notifications
consistently. So "read" and "unread" are now a single line, which should feel familiar from
other messaging apps.

Open a conversation and it scrolls to where you left off, with a line marking what's new.
Everything above the line is read; everything below it is unread. When you leave, the line
moves automatically based on what you've seen.

![A conversation with a line marking where the new messages start](/img/whats-new/whats-new-line.webp)

- **Move it yourself** by pressing and holding a message and selecting **mark as unread**.
- **Play everything unread** with the play button in the lower left — the line moves when it
  finishes.
- **Summarize what's new** with the **Catch-up** button on the line itself.

## Listen Later

You can't always listen to a long voice message the moment it arrives. A long swipe from
right to left labels a message **Listen Later** so it's easy to find again.

![Long-swiping a message to label it Listen Later](/img/whats-new/listen-later.webp)

An indicator at the top of the conversation shows when you have messages saved for later.
Tap **Listen Later** and you get arrows to move between them.

![The Listen Later indicator at the top of a conversation](/img/whats-new/listen-later-indicator.webp)

## Catch up on a walk

A morning walk or commute is a good time to clear the backlog. Long-swipe from right to left
to play all your Listen Later messages along with the new ones — the normal play button always
plays new messages only.

Tap the filter button above the conversation list to see the conversations that need your
attention.

![Filtering the conversation list down to what needs attention](/img/whats-new/catch-up-filter.webp)

## Send from voice memos

Pre-recorded messages have been replaced by the ability to reply and send from voice memos,
which improves stability and removes some redundant functionality. You can still:

- Save a sent message to use again — now **Save to Voice Memos**.
- Reply to a message with something you already recorded — now **Reply with Saved Voice
  Memo**.

The upside is that you can send from *any* voice memo, and organize saved messages into
folders.

If you used pre-recorded messages:

1. Your existing ones are in a folder inside Voice Memos.
2. Zapier workflows that send a pre-recorded message still work, though we'd suggest pointing
   them at the voice memo message ID instead.
3. If a saved message is missing, get in touch.
