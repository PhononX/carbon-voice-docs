# Control what tapping a message does

You told us it was too easy to start playback by accident in a conversation. This release
takes that apart in three pieces.

<!-- truncate -->

## Show More just expands now

It used to be easy to tap **Show More** and start playback instead. The tap area around it
is now much larger, so that shouldn't happen.

## A play button on message cards

Audio messages have a new play button in the lower left. It makes audio messages easy to
spot, and gives you somewhere to tap when you've turned tap-to-play off.

## Choose your tap action

You can now decide what tapping a message card does:

- **Play Message** — tapping a text or audio message starts playback, as before.
- **Open Message** — tapping opens message details instead. Text messages can be played
  from inside the details, and **Play All** still catches you up on everything unread,
  text included.
- **Do Nothing** — tapping the body of the card does nothing. Swipe right to left to open
  the message, or tap the play button in the lower left to play audio.

Change it in **Settings → Conversation History → Message Tap Action**.
