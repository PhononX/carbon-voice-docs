# Find people by username

Heard this one before? "I downloaded Carbon Voice — how do I find you in the app?"

You can now be found by your Carbon Voice username, which makes it simple for anyone to
find and message you.

<!-- truncate -->

## How it works

1. Tell your contact to tap the **+** icon in Carbon Voice.
2. They type your username, like **@travis**.
3. That's it.

If you already created a [Personal CarbonLink](/account-and-settings/personal-carbonlink),
your CarbonLink handle is your username. If not,
[claim your username](/account-and-settings/claim-your-username) — it takes about 30
seconds.

Your contacts can also still reach you through:

- Your registered email or phone number
- Your CarbonLink URL, like `cv.chat/travis`
- Replying to voice memos you've shared
