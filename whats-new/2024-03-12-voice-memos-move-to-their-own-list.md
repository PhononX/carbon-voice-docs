# Voice memos move to their own list

We've moved voice memos to pave the way for some enhancements we're looking forward to
sharing. If you use voice memos a lot, you'll notice they now go to a simple list rather
than into a conversation.

<!-- truncate -->

> Available in version 1.2.14914 and later. Update the app to get it.

Recording and finding them is still quick:

- **To record a new voice memo**, tap the **+** icon at the bottom of the home screen
  and select **Record Voice Memo**.
- **To find your voice memos**, open the **Profile Menu** and choose **Voice Memos**.

Looking for memos you recorded before the change? Tap the **Search** icon at the bottom
left of the home screen and search for the **Me** conversation — the older ones are all
still there.
