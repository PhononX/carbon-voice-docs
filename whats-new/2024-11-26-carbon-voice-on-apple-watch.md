# Carbon Voice on Apple Watch

Record your thoughts wherever life takes you — out for a run, between meetings, or
sorting out your head before bed.

<!-- truncate -->

With Carbon Voice on Apple Watch you can:

- Record a voice memo with a single tap using the Carbon Voice complication.
- Record even when you're offline.
- Sync seamlessly with the mobile app.

Update to the latest version of the app to get it. Access to full conversations from the
Watch is coming.

## Refine on your phone

Capture on your wrist, refine on your phone. Recordings from the Watch land in your voice
memos, where [AI Actions](/ai/transform-with-ai-actions) can turn them into something you
can use.

## Share from your wrist

Share a link by iMessage or email, or send the memo straight to a Carbon Voice
conversation.
