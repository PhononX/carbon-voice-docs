# Send messages to any email address

**Speak your email.** You've been able to send a Carbon Voice message to any email address
for a while — this makes it much better for the person receiving it.

<!-- truncate -->

![A Carbon Voice message as it arrives in an email inbox](/img/whats-new/voice-message-as-email.webp)

**Full transcript, right up top.** The transcript is now in the body of the email, so the
recipient can read your message instantly. It's like sending an email by speaking.

**Clearer playback.** The play button is clearer and sits at the top, so hearing your voice
is one tap away.

**Easy replies.** One click and they're replying to your message.

To send one:

1. Tap the **+** button on the bottom-left chat tab.
2. Enter one or more email addresses, then record your message.
