# Read status, one-tap AI summaries, and Distracted Mode

Your feedback inspired some big changes. This release adds the things you've come to
expect from any communication tool, plus a few that only make sense in a voice-first
one.

<!-- truncate -->

## Read and listen status

You can now see who has caught up on a message — whether it's been seen, heard, or
both. Open message information by pressing and holding a message card and selecting
**Message Info**, by tapping the message's timestamp, or by tapping **⋯** from the
in-line player.

![Message information showing engagement, notes, labels, and transcription](/img/whats-new/detailed-message-info.webp)

Someone is **Caught Up** on a message once they've either cleared its notification or
listened to it. A solid purple check mark appears next to their name when they are.

In the conversation feed:

- A **gray check mark** means at least one person is caught up.
- A **solid purple check mark** means everyone in the conversation is.

![Gray and purple check marks beside messages in a conversation](/img/whats-new/caught-up-check-marks.webp)

## One-tap AI summaries

Tap the **AI** button in the top-right of any conversation to get a summarized version
of its messages, so you can grasp the main points without reading everything. If one
message deserves a closer look, tap **Show transcription** on it to see the full text.

## Expand all transcriptions at once

Messages can be long. Tap the **expanding arrows** at the top of a conversation to
expand every message card and read the whole conversation without tapping "show more"
on each one. The same icon collapses them all again.

## Message previews on the home screen

Conversation cards now show a preview of the most recent message sent, so you can get a
snapshot of what's waiting without opening anything.

![Conversation cards on the home screen showing message previews](/img/whats-new/home-screen-message-preview.webp)

## In-line playback

Tap a message in a conversation to play it right there in the flow of the conversation.
From the in-line player you can:

- **Switch to Distracted Mode** with the button that floats across the top of the
  conversation.
- **See detailed message info** by tapping **⋯** — full transcript, labels, and
  engagement stats.
- **Reply** with the microphone icon, or swipe right to left on the message to switch to
  text.

Tap the **✕** on the player to close it and send a new top-level message instead of a
reply.

## Distracted Mode

Distracted Mode is a simplified player with larger buttons and a decluttered screen,
built for the times you're multitasking and can't focus on your phone.

To get in, tap **Play Notified** on any conversation card from the home screen, or tap
the Distracted Mode icon at the top of a conversation. While you're there, tap **⋯** for
message details, or apply a **Follow-up** label with the label icon beside it so you can
find the message again later.

![Applying a follow-up label from the Distracted Mode player](/img/whats-new/follow-up-label-distracted-mode.webp)

To get out, tap **✕** to exit and stop playback, or tap the Distracted Mode icon to
return to the in-line player with the message still playing.

## Easier conversation navigation

Open a conversation with notified messages and tap the smart navigation arrow in the
bottom-right to jump to the oldest notified message, so you can start catching up
without scrolling to find your place. Tap it again to jump to the most recent message.

The arrows at the top of the feed move you between messages one at a time — useful for
working through notified messages without endless scrolling.

## Notification inbox

Your inbox is where you're notified about important messages, workspace and conversation
invites, and other updates. Find it by opening the **Profile Menu** and tapping the
alert bell in the top-right — both show a red dot when something new is waiting.

It's organized into **All**, **Workspace**, **Mentions**, **Carbon Voice**, **Security
Updates**, and **General**.

## A few things moved

Because this release made room for more messages on screen, some controls changed
places:

- **To pause playback**, tap the message card.
- **To reply with text**, swipe right to left on the message.
- **The full-screen player** was reimagined as Distracted Mode, with message details now
  behind the **⋯** on either player.
