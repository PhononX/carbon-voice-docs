# Build a presentation by talking

Whether it started as a team discussion, a voice memo on the go, or a brainstorm, the
Google Slides add-on takes you from idea to a draft presentation.

<!-- truncate -->

📺 [Watch: from idea to deck](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P3UBLMwWRMI)

## How it works

1. Record your thoughts, or have the discussion, in Carbon Voice.
2. Run the **Presentation Outline** AI Action to structure them — on a single voice memo
   or on a group of messages in a conversation.
3. Open the Google Slides add-on to turn that outline into a draft presentation, with
   visual suggestions and recommendations for refining your message.

[Get Carbon Voice for Google Slides](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/carbon_voice/429860900230)
