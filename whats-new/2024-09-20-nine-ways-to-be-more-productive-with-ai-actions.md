# Nine ways to be more productive with AI Actions

**Let Carbon Voice write your first draft.** AI Actions let you speak an idea and get
structured text back, from a voice memo or from any message in a conversation. There are
hundreds of possibilities — here are nine our own team keeps coming back to.

<!-- truncate -->

Each one gets better with automation: connect [Zapier](/integrations/zapier) and the
output lands in the tool where you actually need it.

## 1. Sales activity and CRM updates

Explain what happened in a meeting and what comes next, and get it structured as a CRM
update you can paste into Salesforce or anywhere else. With automation, it loads into
Salesforce, Pipedrive, Google Sheets, or Notion on its own.

📺 [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gEXmlYyM3lE)

## 2. A to-do list

Build your list whenever a task comes to mind, just by speaking. Carbon Voice structures
it into a neat list you can copy or share — no more waiting until you can stop to write
things down. Automation sends it to Things, Asana, Todoist, or Google Docs.

📺 [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=usTtLr9H2j4)

## 3. A presentation outline

Talking through the next big presentation with a teammate? Take any message in the
conversation, or speak your own thoughts, and run the **Presentation Outline** action for
slide titles, content, and visual recommendations. Automation drops it into Notion or
Google Docs to edit.

📺 [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T2l72zSfGdY)

## 4. An email draft

Need a quick follow-up after a meeting? Speak your thoughts and turn them into a draft to
copy out — or automate it into Gmail or Outlook.

📺 [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qMLB4_1vkGQ)

## 5. A bug report

Spend less time typing up bugs, and get better-written ones from your team. Turn any
message or voice memo into a structured bug with no typing at all, and automate it into
Jira or Asana.

📺 [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V3PABGygqDQ)

## 6. A performance review

Gathering your thoughts for a review and then writing it all out takes a long time. Speak
them instead and get a cleaned-up review organized by strengths and opportunities for
growth. Automation sends the draft to Notion or Google Docs.

📺 [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eJxGW0CXC_I)

## 7. A blog post

Good ideas tend to arrive away from your desk. Record a voice memo and get a solid first
draft written up instead of losing the thought.

📺 [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xTfL9Ry0aLo)

## 8. A LinkedIn post

Same idea, shorter form: record a memo whenever the thought lands and get a post draft
that's ready to edit and share.

📺 [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yAJcdOclxgo)
