# Make your text messages sound like you

By default, a text message you send plays back to the other person in a standard
text-to-speech voice. Clone your voice and it sounds like you spoke it instead.

<!-- truncate -->

## Why use it

- Makes it easy to tell who said what in a conversation.
- Builds trust and clarity by using your own voice.
- Works for both text messages and translated messages.

## How to turn it on

1. Open the profile menu.
2. Tap **Global Voice**.
3. Under **Text-to-Speech**, tap **+ Clone My Voice** and follow the prompts to record a
   short sample.
4. Turn **Voice Cloning** on.

You can switch between your cloned voice and standard text-to-speech at any time in Global
Voice.

## How it works

- Text you send plays back in your voice.
- Translated messages use your voice too.
- With voice cloning off, it falls back to the standard text-to-speech voice you selected.
- You can delete your voice profile at any time from Global Voice.

See [Voice cloning](/ai/voice-cloning) for the full documentation.
