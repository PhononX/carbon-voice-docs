# Connect your AI assistants with the MCP server

If you use Carbon Voice alongside an AI assistant like Claude or Cursor, you can now ask
those tools to analyze and synthesize your conversations and voice memos **directly**. No
copy-pasting, no exporting — just context.

<!-- truncate -->

> At launch, Claude and Cursor are supported. ChatGPT support is coming, and Windsurf isn't
> supported yet.

It turns your conversations into a lightweight knowledge base, which makes it much easier
to reflect, recall, and act.

Things worth trying:

- "Turn my last voice memo in Carbon Voice into a blog post."
- "Based on the Daily Update discussion in Carbon Voice, what is everyone working on? Is
  anyone blocked?"
- "In the Carbon Voice conversation with the XYZ team members, there's a discussion about a
  bug. File it in Jira and assign it to a team member."
- "How can I communicate better with a teammate, based on the messages I've sent them over
  the past month?"

This is a step toward what we're building for: every conversation becoming insight, action,
and relationship intelligence, effortlessly, from your pocket.

See the [MCP documentation](/integrations/mcp) to get set up.
