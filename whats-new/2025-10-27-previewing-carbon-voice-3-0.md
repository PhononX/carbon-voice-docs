# Previewing Carbon Voice 3.0

**Carbon Voice 3.0** is our biggest update yet, available now in preview.

Every conversation holds context, decisions, and next steps — and too often they're lost.
3.0 turns talk into insight, action, and knowledge, so a conversation becomes living
documentation and clear next steps instead of ending. Three new tools make that work: **AI
Chat**, **Action Items**, and **Notebook**.

<!-- truncate -->

## AI Chat

Search and ask questions about your conversations and voice memos in a ChatGPT-style
interface. Things like:

- *"What is James working on?"*
- *"Is the team blocked by anything?"*
- *"Create a marketing plan from this conversation."*

Your conversations become a knowledge base you can query.

You reach AI Chat with the multi-color chat icon in the lower right of a conversation, a
voice memo, or Home — and where you open it decides its scope:

- **From a conversation** — focused on that conversation. Your previous chats about it live
  in the same place.
- **From a voice memo** — answers only from that memo. This history lives in global AI Chat.
- **From a multi-select** — select several messages or memos and the answer draws only on
  those.
- **From Home** — global AI Chat answers across all your conversations and voice memos in a
  workspace, and holds the history of previous global chats and voice memo chats.

AI Chats are constrained to a single workspace, fixed when the chat starts. Results vary
with the number of messages and how the prompt is structured — tell us when something
doesn't match what you expected.

## Action Items

AI finds the key takeaways in conversations and voice memos and turns them into a trackable
to-do list. Assign them, mark them complete, or push them to Asana, Notion, or whatever
project manager you already use with the [Zapier integration](/integrations/zapier).

- **Find them** by asking AI Chat to pull action items out of your conversations.
- **Conversation action items** live behind AI Chat or Conversation Settings.
- **Create your own** with the **+** on the Action Items screen.
- **Save them** from a system prompt: prompts that generate action items, like Meeting
  Summaries and To-do Lists, have a **Save Action Items** button.
- **My Action Items** in Notebook holds everything assigned to you, plus anything you
  created and haven't assigned.

## Notebook

A command center for everything discussed. In one place:

- Voice memos — the home for messages you record outside a conversation
- Action items assigned to you
- Your contact list
- Labeled messages — press and hold a message to set a label

Personal notes and saved AI outputs — blog drafts, meeting summaries, strategy documents —
are coming.

## 3.0 is just the beginning

The goal is simple: make every conversation automatically actionable, turning talk into
knowledge that moves work forward. Help shape what comes next from **Profile → Help → Share
Feedback**.
