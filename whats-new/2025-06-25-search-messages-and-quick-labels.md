# Search messages and quick labels

Two features that make finding and organizing your messages much faster.

<!-- truncate -->

## Search your messages

Find any message by searching its transcript — just type your keywords.

- **Across the app:** from the Home screen, pull down or tap the search icon.
- **Within one conversation:** open the conversation, tap its name at the top, then tap the
  search icon on the right.

Two things worth knowing:

- **Multiple words:** separate them with spaces, like `meeting agenda`.
- **Exact phrases:** use quotes, like `"quarterly review sync"`.

## Quick labels with press and hold

Press and hold any message to set a label on it — a faster way to mark something important
or to trigger a [Zapier workflow](/integrations/zapier).

Together with [auto bulleted summaries](./2025-06-11-auto-bulleted-summaries.md), these
address the awkward part of voice: key information is easy to bury in a recording and hard
to organize. Now you can find what was said, mark it for follow-up, and stay on top of a
conversation. Both are available on any premium plan.
