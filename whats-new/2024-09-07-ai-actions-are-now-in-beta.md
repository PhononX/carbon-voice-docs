# AI Actions are now in beta

**AI Actions** turn any voice memo or message into something you can use: an email, a
to-do list, a blog post, a sales summary, a bug report. Capture with voice, structure
with AI.

<!-- truncate -->

![Turning a voice memo into an email, to-do list, or blog post](/img/whats-new/ai-actions-overview.webp)

📺 [Watch: AI Actions in Carbon Voice](https://youtu.be/V3PABGygqDQ)

## Easy as 1, 2, 3

1. Select any voice memo or message.
2. Choose your output.
3. See the result, then copy or share it.

![Choosing an output and seeing the structured result](/img/whats-new/ai-actions-results.webp)

Update your app to make sure you have it.

## Works with Zapier

Send the output of an AI Action straight to thousands of other apps, so an entire
workflow can start with a voice memo. See
[Carbon Voice and Zapier](/integrations/zapier) to set it up.

## What does beta mean?

Isn't all AI in beta? More seriously: this feature came directly from your feedback about
how you were using Carbon Voice transcripts, and we want to hear which other AI Actions
you'd like and how well the current ones format their results.

## Why we built it

We believe voice is the most natural way to capture and share ideas. As more people used
Carbon Voice, a pattern showed up: they were copying transcripts out of memos and
messages and pasting them into GPT tools to turn them into emails, presentation outlines,
and summaries. Making that a single tap seemed obviously better than making everyone do
it by hand.
