# The best meeting is the one that never gets scheduled

The best way to avoid a meeting is to never have it get scheduled in the first place.

We've updated CarbonLinks and shared voice memos — the two simplest ways Carbon Voice
helps you talk with people without putting another meeting on the calendar.

<!-- truncate -->

They work from opposite sides of the conversation:

- **CarbonLinks** let people reach out to you, like an async phone number.
- **Shared voice memos** let you send your thoughts out with an easy way to respond —
  a reply to an email, a text, or a form, as a quick link. An audio Loom.

Both now have a simpler, more streamlined experience, along with new ways to respond.

## 💬 CarbonLinks: now talk *or* type

![A CarbonLink page with a message box that takes typed or recorded messages](/img/whats-new/carbonlink-text-or-voice.webp)

We've simplified the CarbonLink experience and added a new option: text. People can now
type a message to you as well as record one. No app to download, no meeting to book.

Put your CarbonLink anywhere you want people to be able to reach you — your email
signature, your website, your LinkedIn profile, or in place of your calendar link.

[Claim your CarbonLink](https://cv.chat)

## 🎙️ Shared voice memos: easier to hear, easier to answer

![A shared voice memo with playback, transcript, and a reply panel](/img/whats-new/shared-voice-memo-reply.webp)

We've also redesigned what someone sees when they open a shared voice memo. The new page
puts your message front and center, with playback and the transcript easy to reach. When
someone wants to respond, they can react, type a reply, or talk back.

To create a shareable voice memo, record from the home screen and share the link that's
automatically copied to your clipboard.

## 🌐 See them in action

Two people using both, on their own sites.

### Ari Meisel — [talktoari.com](https://talktoari.com)

![Ari Meisel's site, leading with a voice message instead of a booking link](/img/whats-new/talk-to-ari.webp)

Ari uses a link to a shared voice memo to introduce himself in his own voice, and lets
visitors respond directly to what he said.

### Kevin Koym — [speakwithkevin.com](https://speakwithkevin.com)

![Kevin Koym's site with an embedded voice memo and a reply button](/img/whats-new/speak-with-kevin.webp)

Kevin embeds the voice memo on his site, so visitors can hear his perspective with a
quick play button and respond directly. His contact form uses his CarbonLink to let them
start a new conversation.

In both cases, instead of the website leading straight to "book a meeting," it gives
people a way to just start talking.

The Mac app now supports SSO login, alongside Google, Apple, phone, and email.
