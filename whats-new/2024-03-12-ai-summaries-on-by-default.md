# AI summaries are on by default in every conversation

In response to your feedback, AI summaries are now on by default across all
conversations. Any message that has a summary — typically messages longer than 100
characters, on accounts with an AI plan — will be summarized, so you can grasp the
essence of a long message without reading the whole thing first.

<!-- truncate -->

> Available in version 1.2.14914 and later. Update the app to get it.

## Seeing full transcripts

To see one message in full, tap the expanding arrows in the top-right of its message
card. That reveals the complete transcript alongside the AI summary for that message
only.

To expand every message in a conversation, tap the expander arrows in the top-right of
the conversation screen.

To hide summaries for the conversation you're in, tap the AI sparkles at the top of the
feed.

## Turning summaries off by default

If you'd rather always read messages in full, turn summaries off in
[Conversation History settings](/conversations/access-and-sharing/conversation-history-settings)
(**Profile Menu → Settings → Conversation History**).
