# Carbon Voice Help

This is the official documentation for [Carbon Voice](https://getcarbon.app), voice
messaging for your whole team — people and agents alike. Async, transcribed, always on.

Everything here is written as plain Markdown and kept in a public repository, so you
can read it on this site, browse the source on GitHub, or feed it to an AI assistant.

## Start here

- **[Getting Started](getting-started/index.md)** — install Carbon Voice, set up your
  account, and send your first voice message.
- **[Conversations](conversations/index.md)** — how conversations are organized and
  who can take part in them.
- **[Messages](messages/index.md)** — recording, listening, transcripts, and what you
  can do with a message after it is sent.
- **[AI](ai/index.md)** — summaries, transcription, and the AI features that turn a
  conversation into something you can act on.
- **[Integrations](integrations/index.md)** — connecting Carbon Voice to the other
  tools and agents you work with.
- **[Troubleshooting](troubleshooting/index.md)** — fixes for the problems that come
  up most often.

repository under a [CC BY 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/),
which means you are free to quote, translate, and reuse it with attribution.

Found something wrong or missing? Every page has an **Edit this page** link at the
bottom that takes you straight to the Markdown source.
