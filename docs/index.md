# Carbon Voice Help

This is the official documentation for [Carbon Voice](https://getcarbon.app), voice
messaging for your whole team — people and agents alike. Async, transcribed, always on.

## Start here

- **[Getting Started](getting-started/index.md)** — record your first message and find
  your way around the app.
- **[Conversations](conversations/index.md)** — creating conversations, organizing your
  list, and controlling access.
- **[Messages](messages/index.md)** — recording, sending, listening, and transcripts.
- **[Voice Memos](voice-memos/index.md)** — your own recordings, organized and shared.
- **[AI](ai/index.md)** — summaries, catch-up, transformation, and translation.
- **[Workspaces](workspaces/index.md)** — setting up and running a workspace for your
  team.
- **[Account & Settings](account-and-settings/index.md)** — profile, notifications,
  devices, and plan.
- **[Integrations](integrations/index.md)** — Zapier, AI assistants, and access tokens.
- **[Troubleshooting](troubleshooting/index.md)** — when something isn't working.
