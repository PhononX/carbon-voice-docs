# AI Agents

An **AI agent** in Carbon Voice is an account that belongs to an AI rather than a person.
It has a name, a username, a picture and a voice, it can join conversations, and it can
send and receive messages — so the agent you already run somewhere else becomes something
you can just talk to.

The point is that you stop context-switching. Instead of opening a terminal or a chat
window to reach your agent, you send it a voice message from wherever you already are —
your phone, your watch, a keyboard shortcut on your Mac — and it replies in the
conversation.

## How it works, in three steps

1. **Create an agent Voice account.** Carbon Voice creates the account and gives it an
   identity of its own. See [Create an agent](create-an-agent.md).
2. **Connect your agent.** Link that account to an agent or workflow you've already set up
   — Hermes, OpenClaw, Claude Code, an n8n flow, something you wrote yourself. See
   [Connect your agent](connect-your-agent.md).
3. **Talk to your agent.** Once connected, it behaves like any other member of the team.
   See [Talk to your agent](talk-to-your-agent.md).

If you haven't built an agent yet, you can still do step 1 and come back — the account
works fine on its own until you're ready to connect something to it.

## Where to find them

Open **AI Agents** from the profile menu, or — on desktop — the robot icon in the
navigation sidebar. Agents you've created are listed under **Personal Agents**.
