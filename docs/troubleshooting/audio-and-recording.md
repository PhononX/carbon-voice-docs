# Audio and recording problems

Most recording and playback trouble comes down to permissions, hardware, or a network
that dropped mid-message.

## Carbon Voice will not record

Check these in order:

1. **Microphone permission.** Carbon Voice needs microphone access from your phone's
   settings. If it was denied when you first recorded, it stays denied until you change
   it there — see [Install Carbon Voice](../getting-started/install-carbon-voice.md).
2. **Another app is using the microphone.** A call or another recording app can hold
   the microphone exclusively. Close it and try again.
3. **A connected headset.** If Bluetooth headphones are connected, your phone may be
   recording from their microphone rather than the one you expect.

## A message will not play

1. **Check your volume and silent switch.** On iOS the silent switch can affect
   playback.
2. **Check your connection.** Audio that has not finished downloading cannot play.
3. **Try another message.** If everything fails to play, the problem is your device or
   connection; if only one message fails, the recording itself may be incomplete.

## A message has no transcript

Transcription runs after a message is sent, so a new message can arrive before its text
does. If a transcript has not appeared well after the message did, see
[Transcripts](../messages/transcripts.md).
