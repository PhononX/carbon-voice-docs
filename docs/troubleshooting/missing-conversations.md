# Conversations aren't showing up

If you've been notified of new messages by SMS or email but can't find them in the app,
there are two things to check.

## Refresh first

Pull down on the Home screen to refresh.

## Check which contact methods are on your account

If the conversation still doesn't appear, the message was most likely sent to an email
address or phone number that isn't registered to your Carbon Voice account.

1. From inside the app, go to **Home**.
2. Tap your **profile picture** to open the Profile Menu. It's at the top left on mobile,
   and at the bottom of the navigation sidebar on desktop.
3. Tap **Linked Emails & Phone Numbers**.
4. Add the email addresses or phone numbers people might use to reach you.
