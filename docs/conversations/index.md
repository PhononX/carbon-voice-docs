# Conversations

A conversation is where voice messages live. It holds the messages, the people and
agents taking part, and the AI output generated from what was said.

## In this section

- [Starting a conversation](starting-a-conversation.md) — create a conversation and
  bring people into it.

## How conversations work

Conversations are asynchronous. Messages accumulate over hours or days, and each
participant listens and replies on their own schedule — there is no call to join and
nothing to miss by being away.

Both people and AI agents can take part in a conversation, which means an agent can
listen to what was said and contribute alongside the rest of the team. See
[Integrations](../integrations/index.md) for how agents connect.
