# Start a conversation with a phone number or email

You can start a conversation with anyone using just their email address or phone
number — even if they don't use Carbon Voice yet.

1. From the Home screen, tap the **"+"** icon at the bottom and choose **New
   Conversation**.
2. Choose whether to send a **direct message** or create a **named conversation**, and
   which workspace it should live in. If you don't specify a workspace, you'll find the
   conversation in your personal workspace.
3. Enter the email address or phone number of the person you want to message. If the
   conversation is part of a workspace, you can add someone who isn't a workspace
   member as a **guest**.
