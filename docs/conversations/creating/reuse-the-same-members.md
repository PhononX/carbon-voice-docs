# Create a conversation with the same members

Have a new idea or topic to discuss with the same group of people who are already in
another conversation? You can create a new conversation from an existing one's member
list.

1. Open the conversation and tap the **"..."** in the upper right corner to reach
   **Conversation Settings**.
2. Tap **Members**.
3. Tap the **"..."** in the upper right corner.
4. Select **New Conversation +**.
