# Quick Send

Make a conversation a Quick Send for faster access from Home.

## Add a conversation to Quick Send

1. Press and hold on a conversation to open the menu.
2. Select **Add to Quick Sends**.
3. The conversation appears in the **Quick Sends** section on Home.

<!-- image: quick-send.png — screenshot to re-export from Notion -->

## Using Quick Sends

- **Record a new message** by tapping the mic button on the Quick Send.
- **Open the conversation** by tapping the image.
- **Press and hold** for more options.
