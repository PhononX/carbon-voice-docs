# Press and hold actions

Press and hold on a conversation to get quick options without opening it:

- **View conversation** — open it.
- **Add to Quick Sends** / **Remove from Quick Sends** — pin it to Home for one-tap
  recording.
- **Mark all Read** — only shown when the conversation has unread messages.
- **Copy Conversation Link** — a link you can paste anywhere.
- **Conversation Settings** — access, members, attachments, and everything else.
