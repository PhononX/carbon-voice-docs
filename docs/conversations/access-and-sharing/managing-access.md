# Managing access and adding people

As a conversation owner or admin, you can manage access from Conversation Settings.

## Set who can join

1. Tap the conversation name at the top of the conversation screen.
2. In the **About** section, use the drop-down next to **Who can join**:
   - **Anyone with link** — anyone who has the conversation link can join.
   - **Only workspace members** — anyone in the same workspace or domain can find and
     join.
   - **Only added members** — only people you add can join.

## Add people

<!-- image: add-people.png — screenshot to re-export from Notion -->

1. Tap the conversation name at the top of the conversation screen.
2. Tap the **People** tab.
3. Tap **Add People**, then add someone by name from the list, or by email address or
   phone number.
