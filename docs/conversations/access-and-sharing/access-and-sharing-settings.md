# Access and sharing settings

## Open conversation settings

Tap the conversation name at the top of the screen to bring up the **Settings** menu.
You can also press and hold a conversation on Home and select **Conversation
Settings**.

## Change who can join

Use the **Who Can Join** drop-down to change access. For example, to make a
conversation public so anyone with the link can join, choose **Anyone with Link**.

## Share the conversation link

Tap **Share Link** at the top of the conversation settings screen. You have three
options:

- **Add people directly** to the conversation.
- **Copy the link** to your clipboard, to paste anywhere — a post, an email, a message.
- **Use the share extension** to send the link to a specific destination.
