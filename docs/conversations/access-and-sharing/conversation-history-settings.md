# Conversation history settings

These settings control how messages appear in your conversation feed. All of them live
under **Profile Menu → Settings → Conversation History**.

<!-- image: conversation-history-settings.jpeg — screenshot to re-export from Notion -->

## Managing longer messages

Long messages can be cumbersome to scan, so you can control how much of them you see.

1. Go to **Profile Menu → Settings → Conversation History**.
2. Under **Show only…**, select the character count for message previews.
3. Optionally, toggle on **Don't compress text messages**.

📺 [Watch: managing longer messages](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=czgaLFoWe00)

## AI summaries for longer messages

By default, every message longer than 100 characters gets an instant AI summary, so you
can grasp the main points without reading the full text. Tap the expanding arrows on a
message to read the full transcript, or tap to listen.

To turn AI summaries off across all conversations:

1. Go to **Profile Menu → Settings → Conversation History**.
2. Toggle off **Show AI Summaries by default**.

📺 [Watch: turning off AI summaries](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPeqJzK8vCU)

## Clearing notified messages

You can decide how and when notified messages clear from your conversation feed —
manually for more control, or automatically to keep the feed tidy.

1. Go to **Profile Menu → Settings → Conversation History**.
2. Find **Automatically Clear Notifications from Conversation Feed after…**.
3. Choose a time frame, from never clearing automatically through to various intervals.

If you choose anything other than **Never**, a notified message clears once you've spent
that much time on it.
