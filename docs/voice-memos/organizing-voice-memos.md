# Organizing voice memos

Labels and folders help you find a memo again without scrolling through a long list.

## Labels

Labels keep track of important memos, and labeled memos show up in your feed for quick
access.

1. Tap a voice memo to open more options.
2. Select one of the default labels, or create your own by tapping **"+"**.
3. Find all labeled memos in your list.

AI actions also appear in your list, so you can see which AI tools you've used across
your memos.

## Folders

Folders sort memos by workspace.

1. Go to your list of voice memos and tap the **folder icon** at the bottom right.
2. Choose which workspace to create the folder in.
3. Name the folder.

**Move a memo** by pressing and holding it, then **More → Move**, and choosing a
folder.

**Record straight into a folder** by swiping left to right on the folder.
