# Share a link to a voice memo

You can share a link to a voice memo that lets someone listen to it and reply.

1. Record a voice memo using the mic button on Home.
2. Tap **Share Voice Memo**.
3. Tap **Copy Link**.

📺 [Watch: sharing a voice memo link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rk3YgSnx4vU)

![A shareable link copied automatically after recording a voice memo](/img/whats-new/faster-voice-memo-sharing.webp)

> When you finish a memo, a shareable link is copied to your clipboard automatically, along
> with some text you can customize. Often you can just paste.

## Embedding it on a website

A voice memo and its transcript can be embedded on a website, a blog, or a Notion page.
Paste the shareable link into Notion and it embeds, or take the embed code from message
details for anywhere else.
