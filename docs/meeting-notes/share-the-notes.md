# Share the notes

A write-up nobody reads is the same as no write-up. These are the ways out of Carbon Voice.

## Into another conversation

**Forward** the summary or the key messages into the conversation where the decision
matters — a team channel, a stakeholder thread. Recipients can listen even if they weren't
in the original discussion, and a link back to the source is included so they can see the
context.

See [Message forwarding](../messages/recording-and-sending/message-forwarding.md).

## As text

Copy the transcript or the AI output to your clipboard and paste it anywhere. Every AI
result is also kept in **Notebook → AI Outputs**, so you can come back for it later.

## Into your notes app

The **Obsidian sync plugin** pulls conversations and voice memos into an Obsidian vault as
Markdown — transcripts, AI summaries, and participant links included. If your team keeps
decisions in a knowledge base rather than a chat tool, this is the path.

See [Obsidian](../integrations/obsidian.md).

## Automatically

Labels and conversations can trigger workflows, so the write-up files itself:

- Post the summary into a conversation named **Blog Posts** and get a draft in Google Docs
  or Notion.
- Add a **Bug Report** label and get a Jira issue.
- Add a **CRM Update** label and get a note in Salesforce or Pipedrive.

See [Automate workflows](../integrations/automate-workflows.md) and
[Zapier](../integrations/zapier.md).
