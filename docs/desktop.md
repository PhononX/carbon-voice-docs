# Carbon Voice on desktop

Carbon Voice runs on macOS and Windows as well as on your phone and in a browser. It's the
same app and the same account, but on a wide screen it lays itself out differently, and a
few things live somewhere else as a result.

[Download it](https://www.getcarbon.app/download) if you haven't yet.

## The three-panel layout

On a window wider than about 1100 pixels you get three panels side by side:

1. **The navigation sidebar** — icons for each part of the app.
2. **The list panel** — the contents of whatever you've selected: your conversations, your
   voice memos, your notebook.
3. **The main panel** — the conversation or item you're working in.

Narrow the window and it falls back to the mobile layout, which is also what you get on a
phone-sized browser window.

## Where things are

| | Mobile | Desktop |
|---|---|---|
| Navigation | Bottom bar: Conversations, Notebook, record, Inbox, AI | Left sidebar: Conversations, Voice Memos, Playlists, Contacts, Notebook, AI Agents, Inbox |
| Profile menu | Your picture, top left of Home | Your picture, bottom of the sidebar |
| Search | Icon top right, or pull down | **⌘K**, or the search bar at the top |
| New conversation | The **"+"** on Home | **⌘N** |

## What behaves differently

- **Conversation settings open as a panel** beside the conversation, rather than as a sheet
  over it. Tap the conversation name again to close it.
- **Recording happens in place** in the main panel, instead of taking over the screen.
- **AI Chat has its own button** in the conversation's app bar, rather than living in the
  summarize menu.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut | Does |
|---|---|
| **⌘K** | Open search |
| **⌘N** | New conversation |
| **Space** | Pause or resume recording — or play/pause the player when you're not recording |
| **Enter** | Send the recording you're making |
| **Esc** | Close search, then the side panel, then the player |
| **⌘⇧⌃←** / **⌘⇧⌃→** | Move between the three panels |

Once a panel has focus, the arrow keys move through it and **Enter** opens what's selected.

## Global shortcuts, from anywhere

On macOS there's a second set that works even when Carbon Voice isn't the app you're in, so
you can record a message without switching windows, or send to a conversation from a
keystroke.
That's covered separately in [Quick capture](quick-capture/index.md).
