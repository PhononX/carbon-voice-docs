# Auto bulleted summaries

Auto bulleted summaries turn each message in a conversation into clean bullet points
automatically, so you don't have to open message details one by one. They're most
useful for catching up on longer conversations.

<!-- image: auto-bulleted-summaries.gif — animation to re-export from Notion -->

## What it does

- Converts each message into a bulleted summary.
- Displays the bullets inline as you scroll through a conversation.
- Makes the key points easier to scan.

## Turning it on

1. Open any conversation.
2. Tap the **bulleted summary icon** at the top right, next to the AI toggle.

Messages then appear as bullet points as you scroll. New messages over 10 words or 50
characters are summarized automatically.

## Things to know

- This feature requires a **premium account**.
- Bulleted summaries use **AI credits**.
- You can turn the view on or off at any time.

## Proactive summaries

On a **premium workspace**, summaries can be generated the moment each message is sent,
rather than as you scroll. [Contact the team](mailto:sales@getcarbon.app) to have it
enabled.
