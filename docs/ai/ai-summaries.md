# AI Summaries

A summary turns a conversation you did not have time to listen to into something you
can read in under a minute — what was discussed, what was decided, and what someone
needs to do next.

## When a summary is useful

- Catching up on a conversation that ran while you were away.
- Capturing decisions from a long thread so they are not buried in audio.
- Handing context to someone who joined the conversation late.

## How summaries are generated

Summaries are generated from the [transcripts](../messages/transcripts.md) of the
messages in a conversation, so a message that has not finished transcribing yet is not
part of the summary.

## Limitations

A summary is a compression of what was said, and compression loses detail. Treat it as
a way to find the part of the conversation you need rather than as a replacement for
the conversation itself. When something matters, the transcript and the original audio
are both still there.
