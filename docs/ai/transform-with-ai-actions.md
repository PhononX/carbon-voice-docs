# Transform voice memos and messages with AI

Talking is roughly four times faster than typing. AI actions turn a discussion or a
voice memo into first-draft content: an email, a to-do list, a blog post, a sales
summary, a bug report, a product plan.

The shape of it is always the same:

1. **Capture with voice** — speak naturally and get your thoughts out.
2. **Structure with AI** — turn those thoughts into a first draft.
3. **Share with anyone** — send the structured content wherever it needs to go.

![Ai actions](/img/ai/ai-actions.webp)

## Transforming a single voice memo

1. **Record your voice memo** as usual.
2. Go to the **Voice Memos** tab and find the memo you want to transform.
3. **Tap the memo** to open its message details.
4. **Select an AI action** from the drop-down at the top of the screen.
5. **Copy or share the output** once it's generated.

## Transforming several messages at once

To turn a discussion or a set of voice memos into a presentation outline, a meeting
summary with action items, or anything else:

1. Press and hold any voice memo or message in a conversation.
2. Tap **Select** to choose multiple messages.
3. Choose your AI Magic prompt.

## Finding past results

To see previous AI Magic results across a conversation, tap the **AI Magic icon** in
the bottom right of the conversation screen for the message AI history.
