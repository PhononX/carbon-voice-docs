# One-tap AI summaries

When you need a quick overview, one-tap AI summaries give you the main points of a
conversation without reading through everything.

## Getting a summary

Inside any conversation, tap the **AI** button in the upper right corner. You'll get a
summarized version of every message longer than 100 characters.

## Reading the full message

If something catches your interest, tap the expanding arrows on that message to reveal
the full transcript.

## Turning summaries off

AI summaries are on for all conversations by default. To turn them off, go to **Profile
Menu → Settings → Conversation History**. See
[Conversation history settings](../conversations/access-and-sharing/conversation-history-settings.md).
