# Connect a domain to your workspace

Connecting a domain makes it easier for teammates to find and join your workspace. When
someone logs in with an email address matching your connected domain, they see joining
options based on the rules you set.

<!-- image: workspace-discovery.png — screenshot to re-export from Notion -->

## Join preferences

You choose what happens when someone with a matching domain signs in:

- **Do not inform** — no notification; you add members manually.
- **Auto-join** — anyone with a matching domain is added automatically.
- **Request to join** — they can request access, and an admin approves it.
- **Custom instructions** — show your own message explaining how to join, such as
  pointing them at an internal form or a Slack channel.

## For a new workspace

Step 2 of workspace creation guides you through associating your company domains and
choosing these settings. See
[Create a workspace for your team](create-a-workspace.md).

## For an existing workspace

1. Go to your **Profile Menu** in the bottom-right corner.
2. Tap **Spaces**, then the **gear icon** to the right of your workspace.
3. In Workspace Settings, tap **Admin Controls**.
4. Select **Workspace Discovery**.
5. Tap the **➕** button to add a domain.
6. Use the dropdown to choose the join preference for that domain.
