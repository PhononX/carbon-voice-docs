# Adding someone to a workspace

You can add someone directly, or share a link they can use to join.

1. From the Home screen, tap your profile picture in the bottom right corner to open
   the profile menu.
2. Select **Spaces** to see your list of workspaces.
3. Tap the **gear icon** on the right side of the workspace you want to invite to.
4. Tap **Add People**.

<!-- image: workspace-add-people.jpeg — screenshot to re-export from Notion -->

From there you can either add a contact or create a link to share.

## Adding contacts

Add existing Carbon Voice contacts, email addresses, or people from your phone's
contact list.

## Generating a link

<!-- image: workspace-invite-link.jpeg — screenshot to re-export from Notion -->

A link can be shared directly, posted on an intranet, or put on a website — whoever
receives it can tap it to join the workspace. Two settings control how it behaves:

- **Link expiration** — how long the link stays valid.
- **Single or multi-use** — choose **single use** when inviting one person, and the
  link expires as soon as they join. Choose **multi-use** when a whole team needs to
  join with the same link.
