# Google Workspace

Carbon Voice has add-ons for Google Workspace, so a discussion can land directly in the
document you were going to write anyway.

## Google Sheets

The Sheets add-on imports memos and messages into a spreadsheet, instead of selecting
messages, copying the transcript and pasting it somewhere.

1. [Install the add-on](https://www.getcarbon.app/google-add-on/sheets), or in Google Sheets
   go to **Extensions → Add-ons → Get Add-ons** and search for "Carbon Voice".
2. Choose a conversation, or your voice memos.
3. Set a date range, up to 30 days.
4. Import.

Useful for organizing what came up across a discussion, tracking tasks out of it, or
analyzing a set of conversations together.

## Google Slides

The Slides add-on turns a **Presentation Outline** into a draft deck.

1. Record your thoughts, or have the discussion, in Carbon Voice.
2. Run the **Presentation Outline** AI action on a voice memo or a set of messages. See
   [Transform with AI actions](../ai/transform-with-ai-actions.md).
3. Open the [Slides add-on](https://www.getcarbon.app/google-add-on/slides) and turn that
   outline into slides, with suggested visuals.

## Google Docs

The Docs add-on brings voice into a document two ways:

- **Insert a voice memo transcription** straight into what you're writing.
- **Preview Carbon Voice links as smart chips**, so a link in a doc shows its context
  instead of sitting there as a URL.

## Gmail

The Gmail add-on covers the email side:

- **Attach a voice memo link** to an email.
- **Pull the text out of a memo** to use in the message.
- **Preview the AI actions** you've run on a memo, so you can drop the output in.

## Installing them

- **Sheets, Slides and Docs** come as one listing:
  [Carbon Voice for Editors](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/carbon_voice_for_editors/393337891493).
- **Gmail** is separate:
  [Carbon Voice](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/carbon_voice/65829775758).

All of them are listed at
[getcarbon.app/google-add-on](https://www.getcarbon.app/google-add-on).
