# Share an audio file to Carbon Voice

If you'd rather record somewhere else and get the message exactly right before sending
it, you can share the finished audio file into Carbon Voice.

📺 [Watch: sharing an audio file](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AerJ7r_mtbg)

1. **Record your message** in your preferred recording app, such as Voice Memos on iOS.
2. **Find the share option** in that app. In iOS Voice Memos it's the three dots in the
   upper right corner.
3. **Tap share**, then select **Carbon Voice** from the list.
4. **Choose how to share it** — send the recording as an attachment, or share it
   directly to a conversation.
5. **Search** for the conversation you want if you're sending it to a specific one.
6. **Select the conversation.** Your audio is now posted in Carbon Voice.
