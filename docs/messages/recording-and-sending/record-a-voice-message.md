# How to record a voice message

Recording in Carbon Voice is designed to be fast and intuitive, whether you're
capturing a quick idea or sharing a thoughtful message with your team.

## 1. Start a recording

- Tap the **big purple Record button** at the center of the bottom bar.
- Once you tap, the timer starts, and you're now recording.
- This button is available across most of the app, so you can record from almost
  anywhere.

## 2. While recording

- **Pause** by tapping the mic icon, then tap again to resume.
- **Restart** using the circular arrows icon if it isn't quite right.
- **Delete** the recording in progress by tapping the red trash can icon.
- **Attach a file** while recording — an image, a document, or a link.

## 3. Finish the recording

Tap the **big purple check mark** in the center to complete your recording.

> On the web, make sure your browser allows microphone access. On mobile, check the
> app's permissions in your phone settings if you get an error reaching your mic. See
> [Microphone access](../../troubleshooting/microphone-access.md).
