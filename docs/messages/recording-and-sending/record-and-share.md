# Record and share

There are two ways to record and share in Carbon Voice: **voice memos** and
**conversations**.

## Voice memos

Voice memos live as individual messages. You can share a link for others to listen,
much like sharing a video link. Recipients can reply to the memo, which starts a
conversation with you.

📺 [Watch: recording and sharing a voice memo](https://youtube.com/shorts/rk3YgSnx4vU)

## Conversations

Conversations work like chats, with back-and-forth messages between the people in them.

See [Creating conversations](../../conversations/creating/index.md) to set one up.
