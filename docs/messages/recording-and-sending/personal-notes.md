# Personal notes while you listen

While listening to a message, you sometimes need to gather your thoughts before
replying. Carbon Voice lets you take private notes on a message so you can reference
them as you record your reply.

## Add a note

1. Swipe from right to left on a message to open **Message Details**.
2. Scroll down to **Private notes** and tap to add your note.
3. Tap **Done**, and the note is attached to the message.

## Finding your notes again

Notes appear in the conversation and while you reply to the message. You can also find
them later in the **Notebook** tab.
