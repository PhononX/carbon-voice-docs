# Transcripts

Carbon Voice transcribes every message automatically. That makes a voice conversation
readable — useful when you are somewhere you cannot listen, when you want to skim a
long thread, or when you are looking for something specific that was said.

Automatic transcription is very good but not perfect. Names, jargon, and crosstalk are
the usual sources of error.
