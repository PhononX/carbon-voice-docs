# Clearing and setting notified messages

You can clear the notified flag by hand, set it again on something you want to revisit,
or have it clear automatically as you read.

## On a whole conversation

- **From Home.** Press and hold the conversation card and tap **Clear Notifications**.
- **From conversation settings.** Press and hold the gray section of the conversation
  card on Home, or tap the header in the conversation feed, then choose **Clear
  Notifications**. The same menu has **Set Notifications** to add them back.

![Clear notifications conversation](/img/messages/clear-notifications-conversation.webp)

## On a single message

- **Press and hold** the message and tap **Clear Notification** or **Set
  Notification**.
- **Swipe left to right** on the message.
- **Swipe right to left to reply** — replying also clears the notification.
- **Tap "Clear Notify"** underneath the message in a conversation.

![Swipe to clear notify](/img/messages/swipe-to-clear-notify.webp)

## Clearing automatically

You can have notifications clear on their own as you read through a conversation.

Tap your profile image at the bottom left of Home, then go to **Settings →
Conversation History** and choose your automatic notification timing.
