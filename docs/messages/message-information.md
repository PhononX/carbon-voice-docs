# Message information

Message details show whether your message has been seen or heard, along with its
transcript and everything you can do with it.

## Opening message details

- Press and hold the message card and select **More → Message Info**.
- From the in-line player, tap the **"..."**.
- In distracted mode, tap **See Transcript**.

<!-- image: message-info.jpeg — screenshot to re-export from Notion -->

## What's in it

**Engagement** — who has caught up on your message, either by clearing the notification
or by listening. See
[See if someone has read or listened](read-receipts.md).

**Discussion notes** — take notes as you listen to a message.

**Labels** — flag messages using the default labels or your own, and use them to build
playlists. See
[Playlists and labels](../conversations/your-conversation-list/playlists-and-labels.md).

**Transcription** — read the full transcript, tap a word to jump to that point during
playback, and copy or share the text.

<!-- image: transcript-actions.jpg — screenshot to re-export from Notion -->

**Message actions** — download the message, save it as a pre-recorded message, or
delete it. The last two are available on your own messages only.
