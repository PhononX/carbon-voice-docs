# Attachments

You can attach a file or URL while you're speaking, after your message has posted, or
at the conversation level.

## While you're recording

1. Tap the recorder and start speaking.
2. Scroll up on the recording page until you see the **Attachments** section.
3. Tap the **"+"** to add an attachment.

If you're attaching a URL, copy it to your clipboard first and it should populate
automatically. You can also type one in by hand.

> You can attach while still speaking, or tap **pause**, attach, and tap the mic to
> carry on.

## To a message you've already sent

1. Swipe from right to left on the message to open **Message Info**.
2. Scroll down to the **Attachments** section.
3. Tap the **"+"** to add an attachment.

## To a whole conversation

- When creating a new conversation, the creation flow will offer to attach something.
- For an existing conversation, go to **Conversation Settings** and tap **Media, Links
  and Docs**.

## On the web

The web app supports attachments too. You can attach and send images, video and files such
as a PDF, and download anything someone else attached.
