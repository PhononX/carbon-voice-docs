# Notebook

Notebook collects the things that come out of your conversations, so they're findable
without going back through the conversation they came from.

It's the second tab in the bottom bar on mobile, and in the sidebar on desktop.

![The Notebook, showing a saved AI output generated from four voice memos](/img/messages/notebook.webp)

## What's in it

- **Memos** — your [voice memos](../voice-memos/index.md), including their folders.
- **Action Items** — everything assigned to you, plus anything you created and haven't
  assigned. See [Action items](../meeting-notes/action-items.md).
- **Contacts** — the people you've talked to.
- **Labels** — every message you've labelled, grouped by label. See
  [Playlists and labels](../conversations/your-conversation-list/playlists-and-labels.md).
- **Notes** — the [private notes](recording-and-sending/personal-notes.md) you took while
  listening.
- **AI Outputs** — every result you've generated with an AI action, kept so you don't have
  to regenerate it.

## AI Outputs

This is the one people miss. Every summary, email draft, presentation outline and to-do
list you've ever generated is here, labelled with what it was made from, so a document you
produced weeks ago from a discussion is still one tap away.

Results generated from several messages at once show how many they came from, so you can
tell a summary of one memo from a summary of a whole discussion.

## Pinning what you use

You can choose which sections appear first, so the part of Notebook you actually use is the
part you land on.
