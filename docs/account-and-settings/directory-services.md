# Directory Services — manage your contact methods

Directory Services links multiple email addresses or phone numbers to a single Carbon
Voice account, so whichever contact method someone uses to reach you, the messages all
arrive in one place.

## Adding a contact method

1. Tap your **profile icon** at the bottom right of the navigation bar.
2. Select **Settings**.
3. Tap **Directory Services**.
4. Choose **Add email address** or **Add phone number**.
5. Enter it, and verify if prompted.

Messages sent to any listed address or number then route to your single account.

## When it helps

- Merging duplicate accounts, by linking all their contact methods to one account.
- Anyone using several email addresses or phone numbers.
- Avoiding missed messages by consolidating everything under one account.
