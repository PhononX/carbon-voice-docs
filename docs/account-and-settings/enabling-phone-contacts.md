# Enabling phone contacts

You can connect with your existing contacts who are already on Carbon Voice.

1. From the Home screen, tap the **Profile Menu**.
2. Tap **Find Carbon Voice Contacts**.
3. Tap **Say Hi!** to start a conversation.
