# Get your personal CarbonLink

A personal CarbonLink lets people send you voice messages that you can listen and
respond to whenever suits you — and they can reply whenever suits them, without
downloading an app.

Add it to your email signature, your link-in-bio page, a business card, or anywhere
else your contacts find you. Inside the app it also works like a social media handle,
making you easy to find and message.

## Getting yours

**From the mobile app.** Log in, tap your profile icon at the bottom right of Home,
then tap **Claim your username** and choose your handle. You can customize the look and
feel, add tracking tags, or set a welcome message.

**From the web.** Go to [cv.chat](http://cv.chat) and choose **Get a Personalized
Link**.

Once you have it, add it wherever you share your contact details.
