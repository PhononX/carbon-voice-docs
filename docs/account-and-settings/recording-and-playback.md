# Recording & playback settings

Open your **Profile Menu**, then **Settings → Recording & Playback**.

## How recording starts

By default you can **press and hold** the mic button to record, and release to send. If
that has been producing accidental recordings for you, single-tap recording can be turned
off here.

## Raise to your ear

Turn this on and bringing your phone to your ear starts playing the unread messages in the
conversation, the way answering a call does. Playback pauses when you lower the phone and
picks up again when you raise it.

It's the easiest way to listen privately when you don't have headphones.

## Playback speed

Speed is set per playback rather than in settings. See
[Playback speed](../messages/playback/playback-speed.md).
