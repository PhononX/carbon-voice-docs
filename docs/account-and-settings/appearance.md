# Appearance

Carbon Voice comes in light and dark. By default it follows your device, so it turns dark
when your phone or Mac does.

To choose for yourself, open your **Profile Menu**, then **Settings → Appearance**:

- **Light**
- **Dark**
- **System** — follow the device

On desktop the choice sits inline in the settings list. On mobile it opens a picker showing
each mode.
