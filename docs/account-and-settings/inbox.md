# Notification inbox

Your inbox is where Carbon Voice notifies you about important messages, actions,
workspace and conversation invites, and other updates.

## How it's organized

The inbox is split into sections so it stays manageable:

- **All** — everything across every section.
- **Workspace** — workspace activity: requests to join, name changes, invitations sent,
  people joining, data export requests and their status, and other updates relevant to
  admins.
- **Mentions** — every message where someone has tagged you, in one place.
- **Carbon Voice** — notifications when your contacts join Carbon Voice.
- **Security updates** — login attempts, conversation and workspace name changes, and
  similar.
- **General** — news from the Carbon Voice team, including app updates and articles.
