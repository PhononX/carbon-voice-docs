# Send your first voice message

A voice message is the basic unit of Carbon Voice: you record, it is transcribed, and
the people you sent it to can listen or read whenever they get to it.

## What the recipient gets

Once your message is sent, everyone in the conversation can:

- Listen to the audio.
- Read the [transcript](../messages/transcripts.md).
- Reply with their own voice message, continuing the
  [conversation](../conversations/index.md).

Because Carbon Voice is asynchronous, nobody has to be available at the same time as
you. Recipients are notified and pick the message up when they are ready.
