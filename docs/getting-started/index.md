# Getting Started

Carbon Voice is available on iOS and Android. This section covers everything you need
to go from a fresh install to your first conversation.

## In this section

- [Install Carbon Voice](install-carbon-voice.md) — download the app and create your
  account.
- [Send your first voice message](send-your-first-voice-message.md) — record, send,
  and see what your teammates receive.

## What to expect

Carbon Voice is asynchronous by design: you record when it suits you, and the people
you are talking to listen when it suits them. Every message is transcribed, so a
conversation can be read as easily as it is heard — see [Transcripts](../messages/transcripts.md).
