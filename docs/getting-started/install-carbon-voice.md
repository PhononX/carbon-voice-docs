# Install Carbon Voice

Carbon Voice runs on iOS and Android. Download links for both platforms are on
[getcarbon.app](https://getcarbon.app).

## Install the app

1. Open [getcarbon.app](https://getcarbon.app) on your phone.
2. Follow the link for your platform — the App Store on iOS, Google Play on Android.
3. Install the app and open it.

## Allow microphone access

Carbon Voice needs microphone permission to record. Your phone will ask for it the
first time you record a message; if you decline, recording stays unavailable until you
grant it in your phone's settings.

If you granted permission and recording still does not work, see
[Audio and recording problems](../troubleshooting/audio-and-recording.md).
