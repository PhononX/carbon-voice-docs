# AI Assistants & MCP

The Carbon Voice MCP server lets an AI assistant work with your conversations and voice
memos. The server URL is `https://mcp.carbonvoice.app`.

- [Getting your content to an AI](getting-your-content-to-an-ai.md) — MCP is one of two
  routes; this compares them.
- [What your assistant can do](what-your-assistant-can-do.md)
- [MCP tips and tricks](tips-and-tricks.md)
- [Connecting Claude](claude.md)
- [Connecting ChatGPT](chatgpt.md)
- [Connecting Cursor](cursor.md)
- [Connecting Devin](devin.md)

Your assistant both reads from Carbon Voice and acts in it: alongside finding and
summarizing, it can create voice memos, share links, and action items in your account.
[What your assistant can do](what-your-assistant-can-do.md) covers the whole surface.

For an AI that lives *in* Carbon Voice as a participant, with its own account,
conversations and voice, see [AI Agents](../ai-agents/index.md).
