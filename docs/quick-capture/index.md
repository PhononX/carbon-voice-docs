# Quick Capture

The fastest message is the one you don't have to go looking for the app to send. Everything
in this section does the same job on a different device: get a thought out, to the right
person, without stopping what you were doing.

## On your Mac

- **[Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md)** — record a voice memo or send a message
  from any app, without switching windows.
- **[Speed Dial](speed-dial.md)** — ten slots, each bound to a person, a conversation, a
  folder, or an AI agent, each with its own keystroke.
- **[The menu bar app](menu-bar.md)** — recording and playback from the top of your screen.

## On your phone

- **[iPhone home screen widgets](iphone-widgets.md)** — your recent conversations and
  favorites, one tap from the home screen.
- **[Siri, sharing, and iMessage](ios-shortcuts.md)** — send by voice command, share audio
  in from another app, or send from inside a text thread.

## On your wrist

- **[Apple Watch app](apple-watch.md)** — record from your wrist, even offline.
