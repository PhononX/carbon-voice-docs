# iPhone home screen widgets

There are two widgets you can add to your iOS home screen.

📺 [Watch: iPhone widgets](https://youtube.com/shorts/2o-fajim-wI)

## Recents

Shows your recent conversations with their unread counts, so you can get back to
something quickly. Tapping deep links straight into that conversation.

## Quick Sends

Shows a list of your favorite conversations. Tapping deep links into the conversation.

## Adding a widget

1. Press and hold on your home screen.
2. Tap **Edit** in the top left, then **Add Widget**.
3. Search for **Carbon Voice**.
4. Swipe left and right to choose between the available widgets and sizes, then tap
   **Add Widget**.
5. Tap the card to choose the compact layout, which shows more items.
6. For Quick Sends, configure which of your Quick Send conversations appear.
7. Tap **Done** in the top left to leave edit mode.
